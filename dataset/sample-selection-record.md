# Sample Selection Record

## Aesthetic Lab — Pilot Study V0.1

### 1. Candidate Image Pool

Eight visual design images were collected and reviewed as the initial candidate pool.

The images were compared based on their visible visual characteristics, including composition, typography, color, graphic elements, and expressive techniques.

### 2. Selected Images

Four images were selected for the current exploratory pilot study:

| Image ID | Main Visual Characteristics |
|---|---|
| IMG02 | Grid structure, geometric composition, restrained color palette |
| IMG05 | Geometric construction, strong color contrast, bold visual structure |
| IMG07 | Handwritten typography, irregular shapes, experimental expression |
| IMG08 | Expressionist painting, grayscale treatment, expressive brushwork |

### 3. Selection Rationale

The four images were selected to provide visual diversity.

IMG02 and IMG05 both contain geometric elements but differ in their visual expression and use of color.

IMG07 and IMG08 represent different expressive approaches: handwritten graphic expression and painterly expression.

This combination provides an opportunity to explore whether AI evaluation changes when the same image is assessed under different communication goals.

### 4. Experimental Design

Each selected image will be evaluated under two standardized design goals:

- G01 — Contemporary Cultural Exhibition
- G02 — Urban Youth Music Festival

This produces a total of sixteen planned AI evaluation records: four images × two design goals × two AI models (ChatGPT and DeepSeek). Both models will receive the same images and the same official evaluation prompt to support a consistent comparison.

### 5. Sampling Limitations

This is a small, exploratory sample rather than a representative dataset.

The images were selected through researcher-led visual screening. Their style descriptions are provisional and are not treated as verified ground-truth labels.

The selection does not establish in advance which image is more suitable for either design goal.

### 6. Research Integrity

- Original image files are preserved separately.
- Selected images are copied rather than moved.
- Copyright and source permissions must be checked before publicly redistributing image files.
- No AI evaluation scores or experimental findings are reported in this document.
