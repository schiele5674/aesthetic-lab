# V01 — “A quiet morning in the city”

**Format** A1 portrait, 594 × 841 mm, full bleed (3 mm). No border, no frame, no keyline. Two flat fields meet along one edge; everything is decided by placement against those two fields.

**Viewpoint** Eye level 1.05 m, 50 mm equivalent, tilted 2° down. The camera sits at a table, close to a large window; the window frame falls outside the crop. There is nothing in the picture except a tabletop, a mug, and the far city.

The window/table edge runs the full width at y = 640 (76% of height) and is rotated 0.8° clockwise — 6 mm across the trim. The type stays level, so the tilt reads as the room settling, not as an error.

## Main subject

A single bone-white ceramic mug, 68 mm wide, 74 mm tall, with a chipped rim and a thick D-handle on the right. Its base sits at y = 668, 28 mm *below* the window edge — so the mug straddles the two fields. Its lower third is white against near-black: a hard, unmistakable silhouette. Its upper two-thirds is white against an almost equally light field, so the object loses its edge where the light is strongest. Only three marks keep it legible there: the rim ellipse (a 0.4 mm contour), the handle’s inner opening (filled #9A9280), and one soft shadow plane on the mug’s left wall. Inside the rim, a 2 mm ellipse of tea, #B4763A — the only saturated color on the sheet, 0.3% of the area.

## The city

The city is described by four pieces of rooftop furniture, not by a skyline. Right of the mug, along the same edge: a stair bulkhead (66 mm wide, low), a water tank on four hairline legs (78 mm, with two vertical seam lines), and a 1.4 mm antenna mast rising to y = 442 with one 22 mm crossbar. All flat fills, no windows, no drawn detail, no lit rooms. They are paler than the table and darker than the window — a middle value that reads as distance and as sleep.

## Negative space

Three voids, each with a job:

1. **Upper field, y 0–140 (17%).** Cleared entirely; it lets the eye enter before anything is asked of it.
2. **Middle field, y 210–560 (42% of height, full width).** The poster’s silence — 350 mm with no type, no rule, no logo, no texture. Exactly two marks may enter it: the steam hairline and the antenna tip. Everything else is forbidden. This is the rule that holds the design together.
3. **Table plane, y 640–841 (24%).** Empty weight that keeps the top from floating away; it is the reason the poster sits still.

Filled area: roughly 32% (table, city, mug, type). Empty: 68%. The imbalance is the composition, not a leftover.

## Rhythm

Reading right to left along the horizon: antenna (1.4 mm) → 46 mm gap → tank (78) → 40 mm gap → bulkhead (66) → 44 mm gap → mug (68). Gaps of 40–46 mm, masses of 66–78 mm: an almost even pulse, interrupted once by a hairline. That regularity is the calm. Above it, the single vertical of the steam, drifting 8 mm left over 208 mm with one 2 mm kink at a third of its height, gives the only curved gesture in an otherwise orthogonal picture.

## Value and color

Four tones and one accent. No gradient anywhere — the window field is dead flat, because flatness reads as air and a gradient reads as weather.

| Role | Hex | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Paper / window light | #E9E4D8 | 64% |
| Distant city | #B4B0A3 | 3.5% |
| Table, type, ink | #26241F | 30% |
| Steam, mug shading | #8F8878 | 0.6% |
| Tea | #B4763A | 0.3% |

Light comes from the right and slightly behind the mug, 10° above the horizon. It is described only by consequence: the mug’s right wall is blown to paper, the handle’s opening is dark, the city is pale because it is far, and the table is dark because it faces away.

## Typography

- **Title**, two lines, sentence case, with the period: “A quiet morning” / “in the city.” Transitional serif (Tiempos Text, or Source Serif as fallback), 30/40 pt, tracking −0.5%, #26241F, flush left at x = 40. Its thin strokes repeat the steam hairline; its 100 mm measure stops 34 mm short of the mug.
- **Subtitle**, one line: “CITY READINGS · NO. 01 · 06:00–08:00”. Neutral grotesque (Söhne/Inter), 8 pt, caps, tracking +14%, #6B6455, flush left, baseline 196. Its even rhythm repeats the city band.
- No other type. No logo, no sponsor strip, no caption. Type occupies the top-left corner and nothing else.

## Handling

Offset, one black plus one spot sienna, 120 gsm uncoated warm stock. Table plane built as 100K + 4C so it sits matte and does not bronze; 3% noise in the dark field only. No varnish, no gradient mesh, no photographic grain in the window field.

## Exclusions

No clouds, birds, sun disc, lens flare, bokeh, vignette, drop shadows, window frames, curtains, plants, crockery sets, people, vehicles, lit windows, or line-art iconography. No second object on the table. Nothing placed in the middle field.
