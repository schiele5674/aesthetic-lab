# Standardized Experimental Design Briefs
## Aesthetic Lab — Pilot Dataset V0.1

**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Define consistent design goals for visual aesthetic evaluation.

These briefs are experimental evaluation contexts. They do not describe the original intentions of the designers whose works may be selected.

---

## G01 — Contemporary Cultural Exhibition

### Design Context

A contemporary cultural exhibition introducing art, culture, and creative ideas to a general audience.

### Target Audience

Young adults and culturally interested visitors aged approximately 20–40.

### Communication Objective

The visual design should communicate cultural depth and encourage curiosity without overwhelming the viewer.

### Desired Emotional Impression

- Quiet
- Thoughtful
- Restrained
- Cultured
- Contemporary

### Visual Communication Priorities

1. Clear and balanced composition.
2. A coherent relationship between typography and imagery.
3. A distinctive but controlled visual identity.
4. A sense of cultural depth and atmosphere.
5. Appropriate use of negative space, color, and visual hierarchy.

### Evaluation Question

Does this visual design communicate the atmosphere of a contemporary cultural exhibition while attracting the intended audience?

---

## G02 — Urban Youth Music Festival

### Design Context

A contemporary urban music festival featuring young musicians, live performances, and youth culture.

### Target Audience

Young adults and music enthusiasts aged approximately 18–30.

### Communication Objective

The visual design should attract attention, communicate energy, and create excitement about attending the event.

### Desired Emotional Impression

- Energetic
- Youthful
- Exciting
- Bold
- Social

### Visual Communication Priorities

1. Strong visual impact.
2. A clear and memorable visual identity.
3. Dynamic composition or visual rhythm.
4. Effective use of color, typography, and graphic elements.
5. An atmosphere that communicates music and collective excitement.

### Evaluation Question

Does this visual design communicate the energy of an urban youth music festival and motivate the intended audience to engage with it?

## 3. Experimental Consistency Rules

1. Use the exact same G01 brief for all G01 images.
2. Use the exact same G02 brief for all G02 images.
3. Do not modify the briefs after AI evaluation begins.
4. Provide the complete relevant brief to both AI and human evaluators.
5. Do not assume that an existing image was originally designed for either experimental context.
6. Record any deviations from these briefs.

---

## 4. Research Note

The two design goals intentionally have different communication priorities.

The experiment investigates whether evaluators can adapt their aesthetic judgments to these different goals, rather than consistently favoring one visual style.

The briefs are operational definitions for this pilot study, not universal definitions of cultural exhibitions or music festivals.
