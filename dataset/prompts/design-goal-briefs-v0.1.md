# Design Goal Briefs — Aesthetic Lab V0.1

## Purpose

This document defines standardized design goals for the Aesthetic Lab pilot study.

The purpose is to evaluate whether multimodal AI can adjust its aesthetic judgment according to different communication goals, rather than consistently favoring one visual style.

## G01 — Contemporary Cultural Exhibition

**Design Context:**  
A visual poster for a contemporary cultural exhibition.

**Target Audience:**  
Young adults and culturally interested visitors.

**Communication Goal:**  
Communicate a sense of culture, reflection, and quiet sophistication.

**Desired Emotional Qualities:**
- Calm
- Restrained
- Thoughtful
- Culturally expressive

**Evaluation Considerations:**
- Does the visual composition support a calm and reflective atmosphere?
- Is the information hierarchy appropriate?
- Do the visual elements contribute to a coherent cultural identity?
- Does the design avoid unnecessary visual noise?

## G02 — Urban Youth Music Festival

**Design Context:**  
A visual poster for an urban youth music festival.

**Target Audience:**  
Young adults interested in contemporary music and urban culture.

**Communication Goal:**  
Communicate energy, excitement, youthfulness, and a strong sense of participation.

**Desired Emotional Qualities:**
- Energetic
- Bold
- Expressive
- Dynamic

**Evaluation Considerations:**
- Does the visual composition communicate energy and movement?
- Is the visual impact strong enough to attract attention?
- Do typography, color, and imagery support the festival atmosphere?
- Does the design create a recognizable and engaging identity?

## Experimental Rules

1. Apply both G01 and G02 to the same selected images.
2. Do not assume that a particular visual style is automatically suitable or unsuitable for either goal.
3. Evaluate the visible design evidence rather than guessing the original designer's intention.
4. Use the same official evaluation prompt for both goals.
5. Record the goal ID in every AI evaluation record.

## Scope Limitation

This is an exploratory pilot study. The two design goals represent different communication objectives, but they do not represent all possible design contexts.
