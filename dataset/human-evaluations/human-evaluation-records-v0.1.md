# Human Evaluation Records v0.1

## 1. Study Information

- Project: Aesthetic Lab
- Evaluation Date: 2026-11-16
- Evaluator: One human evaluator
- Evaluation Scope: 4 images × 2 design goals = 8 evaluations
- Scale: 1–5
- Evaluation Type: Individual exploratory human evaluation

## 2. Scoring Rubric

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Very poor alignment |
| 2 | Poor alignment |
| 3 | Moderate alignment |
| 4 | Good alignment |
| 5 | Excellent alignment |

Scores represent perceived alignment with the assigned design goal, not universal aesthetic quality.

## 5. Methodological Notes and Limitations

- This dataset contains evaluations from one human evaluator only. It does not represent general human consensus.
- The evaluator was given the same design-goal descriptions and scoring scale used in the study.
- IMG02 was evaluated after the evaluator had already seen AI evaluation outputs for this image. Its human scores are therefore not blind evaluations.
- The other three images were evaluated without prior exposure to their AI evaluation outputs, but the evaluation process included structured goal-related prompts.
- The results are exploratory and should not be generalized beyond this small pilot dataset.
- Original judgments are retained to preserve the evaluator's wording.
