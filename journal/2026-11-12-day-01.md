# Research Journal — Day 01

**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Project:** Aesthetic Lab  
**Status:** Project initialization

## Today's intention

Start a research project exploring aesthetic intelligence in AI across visual, literary, and interaction tasks, while learning the engineering skills needed to build repeatable evaluation workflows.

## Initial hypothesis

AI outputs can satisfy literal instructions while still feeling generic, visually unbalanced, emotionally flat, or socially unnatural. A useful evaluation process should therefore separate instruction-following from aesthetic quality and explain judgments with observable evidence.

This is a starting hypothesis, not a finding.

## Initial research dimensions

- Visual: composition, color, space, style coherence, mood.
- Literary: precision, rhythm, imagery, originality, emotional effect.
- Interaction: intent understanding, naturalness, tact, relevance, adaptability.
- Engineering: reproducibility, data quality, evaluation consistency, safe automation.

## Decisions made

1. Use one repository as the home for research notes, data, code, and results.
2. Preserve raw model outputs and record model/version metadata.
3. Begin with a small exploratory pilot: 15 tasks, two models, 30 outputs.
4. Keep instruction-following separate from aesthetic ratings.
5. Treat early ratings as individual judgments until multiple evaluators and agreement checks are available.

## Questions to investigate

- Which rubric criteria are clear enough for another person to apply?
- How much do aesthetic preferences vary across evaluators?
- Can pairwise preference judgments be more reliable than absolute scores?
- Which evaluation steps can be automated without hiding important context?
- How should visual outputs be evaluated consistently when image generation settings vary?
