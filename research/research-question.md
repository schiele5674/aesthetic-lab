# Research Questions — Aesthetic Lab 0.1

## Main question

How can we evaluate whether an AI output satisfies a human's aesthetic intention, beyond merely checking whether it follows literal instructions?

## Sub-questions

### Visual
- Does the output establish a clear visual hierarchy?
- Are composition, color, space, and style coherent with the brief?
- Does the output convey the intended mood?

### Literary
- Is the language precise and purposeful?
- Does it use imagery and rhythm effectively?
- Does it avoid cliché, padding, and decorative language that does not serve the task?

### Interaction
- Does the response understand the user's likely intent?
- Is its tone natural and appropriately restrained?
- Does it provide the right amount of help without overstepping?

### Evaluation and engineering
- Can evaluators apply the rubric consistently?
- Which dimensions are suitable for automated checks?
- How can an agent collect, organize, and summarize evidence without replacing human judgment?

## Scope of the first pilot

This is a small exploratory benchmark, not a validated scientific instrument. It uses 15 tasks and two selected models. Results should be described as observations under the recorded conditions, not universal rankings.
